Animal Park / Домик у тропы — 2D art direction for a portrait game on the Belarusian floodplain. Relate every shape to a small child's hand: soft worn wood, linen, willow wicker, orchard leaves and dense soft fur. Express volume through local colour planes, subtle pigment grain and a readable outer silhouette. Use muted willow olive, alder grey-green and oat straw, with apple vermilion reserved for interaction. Preserve cool, quiet distance and warmer tactile foreground. Keep small-screen shapes deliberate; irregular branching, modest leaf clusters and soft matte surfaces. Render a single reusable asset with clean edges in neutral diffuse light. No baked cast shadows, rim light, glows, outlines, fog, particles, animated water, lettering, UI, motion blur, backdrop or frame. The Godot scene supplies lighting, water, atmospheric depth, shadows, movement and interaction.
